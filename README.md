# Bank Heist – Video Game Design Team

We are GT Video Game Design Team Encore. This repository contains our **Bank Heist** stealth game built in Unity.

---

## How to Start
1. Switch to the **main** branch.
2. Open **`Assets/Scenes/MainGame.unity`** in Unity.
3. Press **Play** to begin.

---

## Game Overview
You play as a **thief** infiltrating a bank. Your objectives:
- **Find the key** to the bank **vault**.
- **Steal as much money as possible** to increase your score.
- **Avoid detection** by patrolling police officers.

If you’re caught, the heist ends!

---

## Controls (default)
- **W/A/S/D** or **Arrow Keys** – Move
- **Mouse** – Look/rotate (if applicable)
- **Esc** – Pause/Menu

*(Adjust based on your build settings.)*

---

## Tips & Strategy
- Move around to **observe police patrol routes**.
- **Test and learn** their patterns to find safe gaps.
- Plan your path to the vault and loot, then **slip out unseen**.
